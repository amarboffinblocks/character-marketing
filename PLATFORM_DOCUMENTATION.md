# Character Market Platform Guide

This guide provides a comprehensive overview of every page in the platform, its purpose, and its function.

---

## 🌐 1. Public & Discovery
**Explore creators and find inspiration for your roleplay assets.**

| Page | URL | What You'll Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Marketplace Home** | `/` | Browse featured creators and platform highlights |
| **Creator Marketplace** | `/creators` | Search and filter through the full list of available artists |
| **Creator Profile** | `/creators/[creatorId]` | View a creator's portfolio, packages, and full review history |
| **FAQ Page** | `/faq` | Get answers to common platform, asset, and billing questions |
| **Asset Inventory** | `/inventory` | Browse a unified gallery of all public roleplay assets |
| **Saved Creators** | `/saved-creators` | Access a list of creators you have bookmarked for later |

---

## 🛍️ 2. Checkout & Purchase
**Securely hire creators and pay for custom commissions.**

| Page | URL | What You'll Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Custom Package Checkout** | `/creators/[id]/custom-package` | Configure and purchase a custom service from a specific creator |
| **Direct Purchase** | `/creators/[id]/purchase-preselect` | Instantly buy a pre-defined service package |

---

## 👤 3. Customer (Buyer) Dashboard
**Manage your character requests, orders, and communications.**

| Page | URL | What You'll Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **My Bids Dashboard** | `/post-a-bid` | View and manage all your open character requests (bids) |
| **Create New Bid** | `/post-a-bid/new` | Post a new request with budget and specific asset requirements |
| **Edit Bid** | `/post-a-bid/[bidId]/edit` | Update your character request details before a creator is hired |
| **My Orders** | `/orders` | Track the progress of all your active and past commissions |
| **Order Preview** | `/orders/[id]/preview` | Review delivery drafts, request changes, or approve the final work |
| **Customer Messages** | `/messages` | Chat directly with creators about your active orders |
| **Notification Inbox** | `/inbox` | See real-time activity updates for all your bids and orders |
| **Transaction History** | `/transactions` | View all your past payments, refunds, and billing details |

---

## 🎨 4. Creator Dashboard
**Tools for creators to manage their business and fulfill orders.**

| Page | URL | What You'll Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dashboard Home** | `/dashboard/creator` | Get a snapshot of active orders, new proposals, and revenue |
| **Global Bids (Jobs)** | `/dashboard/creator/global-bids` | Browse customer requests and send proposals to win new work |
| **Active Orders** | `/dashboard/creator/orders` | Manage your current jobs and upload deliverables for review |
| **Order Details** | `/dashboard/creator/orders/[id]` | View full requirements, deadlines, and chat for a specific order |
| **Incoming Requests** | `/dashboard/creator/requests` | Accept or reject direct hiring requests from clients |
| **Creator Messages** | `/dashboard/creator/messages` | Communicate with your buyers via real-time secure chat |
| **Reviews & Feedback** | `/dashboard/creator/reviews` | View all customer feedback and post official creator replies |
| **Earnings & Finance** | `/dashboard/creator/earnings` | Track revenue, pending balances, and transaction history |
| **Creator Transactions** | `/dashboard/creator/transactions` | Detailed ledger of all fees, sales, and payouts |

---

## 🏗️ 5. Creator Workspace
**Manage your library of roleplay assets and character data.**

| Page | URL | What You'll Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Character Gallery** | `/dashboard/creator/workspace/characters` | View and manage your entire library of character cards |
| **Create Character** | `/workspace/characters/new` | Build a new character with personality and JSON card data |
| **Avatar Gallery** | `/dashboard/creator/workspace/avatars` | Organize and tag your character portraits and visual sets |
| **Upload Avatar** | `/workspace/avatars/new` | Add new visual assets or avatar packs to your workspace |
| **Lorebook Gallery** | `/dashboard/creator/workspace/lorebooks` | Manage world-info files and character knowledge bases |
| **Create Lorebook** | `/workspace/lorebooks/new` | Build a new lorebook with keyword-triggered context |
| **Background Gallery** | `/dashboard/creator/workspace/backgrounds` | Manage your environment and scene-setting visual assets |
| **Create Background** | `/workspace/backgrounds/new` | Add new environment cards to your collection |

## 🔐 7. Account & Security
**Manage your identity and account preferences.**

| Page | URL | What You'll Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sign In** | `/sign-in` | Log in to your existing Character Market account |
| **Sign Up** | `/sign-up` | Create a new account as a buyer or creator |
| **Forgot Password** | `/forgot-password` | Request a reset link for your account password |
| **Reset Password** | `/reset-password` | Choose a new password via a recovery email link |
| **OTP Verification** | `/otp-verification` | Verify your identity using a one-time passcode |
| **Profile Settings** | `/profile` | Update your display name, bio, and visual profile |
| **Account Settings** | `/settings` | Manage security, sign out from all devices, or delete your account |
